Good Night

Genre : Comedy

Rating : ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Short description :

Starring :

K. Manikandan and Meetha Raghunath.

Plot :

Mohan, an ordinary IT employee, struggles with a severe, room-shaking snoring problem that earns him the nickname "Motor" Mohan. Despite his insecurities, he falls in love and marries Anu, a quiet girl who accepts his condition. The film focuses on the realistic and humorous aftermath of their marriage when his snoring ruins Anu's sleep, leading to a touching and comforting exploration of compromise, insecurity, and love.

Doctor

Genre : Comedy

Rating : ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Short description :

Starring :

Sivakarthikeyan, Priyanka Mohan, and Vinay Rai.

Plot :

Varun is a stoic, unemotional military doctor who is abruptly dumped by his fiance. Right after the breakup, her young niece is mysteriously abducted. Varun assembles an unconventional, quirky team to launch a daring rescue operation, leading them to a sprawling human trafficking syndicate operating out of Goa in this black-comedy thriller.

Kudumbasthan

Genre : Comedy

Rating : ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Short description :

Starring :

K. Manikandan and Saanve Megghana.

Plot :

Set in Coimbatore, this comedy-drama follows Naveen, a graphic designer and the sole breadwinner for his family, which includes his wife and parents. After an ego clash with a client, he loses his job but keeps the termination a secret from his status-obsessed family. The film humorously and emotionally chronicles his desperate schemes to make money while navigating the pressures of middle-class life and marital commitments.
